Frequently Asked Questions

Mount Toubkal (4,167m) is a non-technical climb, meaning no previous mountaineering experience or ropes are required. However, it requires a reasonable level of physical fitness as you will be hiking 5 to 7 hours per day uphill. We recommend regular cardio training like hiking or running before your trip.
We recommend sturdy broken-in hiking boots, warm fleece/down layers, waterproof jacket, sun protection (glasses, hat, sunscreen), sleeping bag, and a daypack. If you don't have crampons or trekking poles, they can easily be rented in Imlil village before the trek.
Yes! All our treks are fully inclusive of certified local mountain guides, cook, muleteers, and mules to carry your main luggage. Freshly cooked traditional Berber meals (breakfast, lunch, and dinner) are prepared daily by our team cook.
Depending on your chosen travel style (Standard, Comfort, or Luxury), accommodation ranges from authentic Berber desert camps and 3-4★ traditional Riads to 5★ luxury glamping tents with private bathrooms, hot showers, and king beds in Merzouga or Erg Chebbi.

You can fill out any booking request form on our website or contact us directly via WhatsApp (+212 653 274 190). No immediate upfront payment is required to submit your request—our team will review your details, confirm availability, and guide you through the secure confirmation process.
