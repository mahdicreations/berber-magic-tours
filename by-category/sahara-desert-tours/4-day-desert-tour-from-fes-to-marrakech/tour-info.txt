ITINERARY:
Day1 : Fes- Atlas Mountains- Ziz valley- Merzouga Desert ,
Your journey begins as your driver picks you up from your riad or hotel at 7:00 am for a trip to Merzouga. The route takes you through the Middle Atlas, passing through Irfan, Midelt, Errachidia, Erfoud, and Rissani. Lunch will be arranged in one of the cities or villages based on the prevailing weather conditions. Throughout the day, you'll traverse forests, rugged rocks, and Berber villages, gradually descending from the Atlas Mountains into the Sahara Desert. Around midday, there will be a break for lunch in the city of Midelt, renowned for its fossils and rocks. Afterward, you'll continue the journey, immersing yourself in the changing landscapes. As you reach the Kasbah of Merzouga, you'll have the chance to freshen up with a hot shower or take a refreshing dip in the pool, depending on the weather conditions. This will be a delightful respite after the day's travels. The evening concludes with dinner served at the Kasbah, promising a satisfying end to your day.
Day2 : Guided sightseeing tour in Merzouga ,
After breakfast, embark on a visit to the village of Khamliya, home to the Gnawas, people of African origin who have settled in the Moroccan desert in pursuit of a better life. Explore the village and gain insights into their unique way of life. Next, head to the city of Rissani for a tour of its bustling souk, where you can immerse yourself in local products. Don't miss the opportunity to savor a Berber pizza known as "medfouna" at a local restaurant. In the afternoon, get ready for a camel ride across the dunes, unveiling the secrets of the vast sea of sand. Your destination is a pristine oasis where you'll have a chance to relish the tranquility of the desert, marvel at the beauty of nature, and witness a breathtaking sunset. The evening unfolds with dinner and an overnight stay in a Berber tent, situated at the base of one of the tallest dunes in Erg Chebbi. It promises to be a serene and enchanting experience under the desert sky.
Day3 : Merzouga- Toudra gorges- Dades Gorges- Ouarzazate ,
Early breakfast and a refreshing shower, set off for Ouarzazate via the Toudra Gorges. Located 14 km from the town of Tinghir, the journey will take you through the village of Boumalen Dades, the picturesque Kalaat Megouna known as the Valley of Roses, and Skoura. Finally, you'll reach Ouarzazate, where dinner and overnight accommodations await you at the hotel. It's a continuation of your adventure through diverse landscapes and cultural experiences.
Day4 : Ouarzazate- Ait Ben Haddou Kasbah- Atlas Mountains- Marrakech ,
After enjoying breakfast, begin your journey towards Marrakech. Before leaving Ouarzazate, you have the option to visit the Kasbah of Taourirte, founded by Pacha Laglaoui. If interested, you can also take a guided tour of the movie studios known as "50 Crazy People," located 20 km from Ouarzazate. Another noteworthy stop is the famous Ait Benhaddou Kasbah, situated 9 km from the national road. The visit typically lasts around an hour, allowing you to appreciate the historical and architectural significance of this ancient fortress. After the visit, continue your journey to Marrakech, enjoying beautiful landscapes along the way. Take a break for lunch at a restaurant along the road. Upon reaching Marrakech, you will be dropped off at your destination, marking the end of your desert adventure. It's a day filled with cultural exploration and scenic beauty as you transition from the desert to the vibrant city.
