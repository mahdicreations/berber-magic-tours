ITINERARY:
Day1 : Casablanca- Rabat- Meknes-Fes,
Your day begins with a pickup from your accommodation in Casablanca at 8:00 am. First, you'll visit the splendid Hassan II Mosque, a breathtaking landmark with the tallest minaret at 210m. Explore the Corniche Ain Diab for scenic views. Afterward, depart for Rabat along the coastline, heading to the political capital of Morocco.
In Rabat, you'll visit the Oudaya Kasbah, an old medina with beautiful narrow streets and small blue homes. Next, explore the Chella Necropolis, where Roman and Islamic ruins coexist, sharing stories of centuries past. Continue your journey to the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis (Meknes). Finally, arrive in Fes, a UNESCO World Heritage Site renowned for its historical significance. You'll spend the night here, experiencing the charm and cultural richness of the city.
Day2 : Fes- Ifrane- Azrou- Eerfoud-Merzouga Desert ,
At 8 am, your day will commence with a hotel pickup, initiating a journey towards Merzouga. The route will traverse the Middle Atlas, a 350-kilometer mountain range stretching from southwest to northwest. During the trip, you'll pass through cities like Ifrane, often referred to as the "Moroccan Switzerland" due to its picturesque architecture, and Azrou, a well-known Berber city in Morocco. The scenic drive continues through the Ziz valley and gorges, with stops in cities such as Midelt and Erfoud.
Upon reaching Erfoud, a desert track will lead us to Merzouga. The day's exploration will culminate in an overnight stay at a hotel near the desert entrance. This journey promises a blend of diverse landscapes, city exploration, and the anticipation of the desert experience awaiting you in Merzouga.
Day3 : Guided tour in Merzouga Desert ,
On the second day, our focus shifts to immersing ourselves in the breathtaking landscapes of the Sahara. The day unfolds with a 4X4 adventure, taking us through nomadic villages, picturesque oases, and the majestic dunes of Erg Chebbi. During the journey, we'll make a stop at the renowned village of Khamlia, inhabited by the Gnawa people—descendants of African slaves—where we'll be treated to their captivating folk music.
As dusk approaches, we'll return to the hotel and embark on a camel ride, guided by the gentle sway of the camels, leading us to a Berber camp nestled in the heart of the dunes. Here, we'll have a delightful dinner under the desert sky and spend the night surrounded by the serene beauty of the Sahara. It's a day marked by exploration, cultural encounters, and the enchanting allure of the desert.
Day4 : Merzouga- Rissani-Tinghir-Todra Gorges- Dades Gorges ,
Begin your day by waking up early to witness the enchanting sunrise over the desert. Following this magical moment, embark on a camel ride back to the dunes. After enjoying a hearty breakfast and a refreshing shower, our journey continues with a drive to Rissani and Erfoud. Explore the local craftsmanship at a fossilized marble factory before proceeding to the palm groves of Touroug and Tinjdad.
For lunch, we'll make a stop at the stunning Todra Gorge, surrounded by breathtaking landscapes. In the afternoon, the journey unfolds as we head to the Dades Gorge via Boumalne. As the day concludes, a delightful dinner awaits, and we'll spend the night at a hotel, allowing you to immerse yourself in the unique charm of the region. It's a day filled with natural wonders, cultural exploration, and the tranquility of the Moroccan landscape.
Day5 : Gorge Dades- Kelaa M’Gouna- Skoura- Ourzazate ,
After a delicious breakfast, we continue our journey to Dades Valley and embark on the road to Boutaghrar, where the breathtaking colors of nature come together. Our journey takes us along the famous route of a thousand kasbahs, allowing us to explore the kasbahs, ksours, the Valley of Roses, and the Skoura palm grove. Along the route, there's an optional visit to the Kasbah Amredhyle.
As the day winds down, we continue our journey to spend the night in a hotel in Ouarzazate. It's a day brimming with vibrant landscapes, historical exploration, and the unique charm of Morocco's kasbahs and valleys.
Day6 : Ourzazate- Ait Ben Haddou-Atlas Mountains- Marrakech ,
Embark on an exploration of the city, renowned for its kasbahs, including the Taourirt World Heritage Site and one of the most beautiful palaces in the region. In close proximity lies the Kasbah of Ouarzazate, where we'll take a pause to visit the cinema museum and film studios. These studios have been the backdrop for numerous international films such as Gladiator, Lawrence of Arabia, Game of Thrones, and Babel. Following this cinematic adventure, we'll travel 20 km and turn right to visit the famous kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou.
Continuing our journey, we'll cross the High Atlas Mountains, unveiling spectacular landscapes and hidden Berber villages nestled at the foot of immense valleys. You have the option for a lunch stop at a local restaurant at the Tichka Pass, or if you prefer to maximize your remaining time, we'll proceed directly to Marrakech. This offers the chance to visit the magnificent Majorelle Gardens. The choice is yours, ensuring flexibility and the opportunity to immerse yourself in the aspects of Morocco that captivate you the most.
