ITINERARY:
Day1: Casablanca- Rabat-Mknes-Fes,
You will be picked up at 08:00am from your accommodation in Casablanca Casablanca, take a short visit to the splendid Hassan II Mosque: a breathtaking landmark of Casablanca with the tallest minaret at 210m, explore the Corniche Ain Diab for scenic views, later on depart Rabat along the coast line to the political capital of Morocco. Visit to the Oudaya Kasbah: Rabat's old medina with its beautiful narrow streets and small blue homes. Then visit the Chella Necropolis where the Roman and Islamic ruins lie side by side whispering endless stories of centuries past. Continue to the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis (Mknes). Arrive in Fes, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its historical significance. Overnight here.
Day2: Fes-Ifrane-Azrou-Midlt- Merzouga Desert,
Today, the journey continues from Fes to Ifrane known as “"Switzerland of Morocco" with its chalet like houses, built during the French protectorate as a hill station, the buildings were designed in a similar style to French Alpine villages. The plants and trees were also imported to give a European feel to the city. The name Irfane means caves and is taken from the local Amazigh language, Tamazight. It is highlighted for its low temperatures, distinctive architecture, various landscapes, lakes, streams and varied outdoor pursuits. We continue to Azrou, explore the cedar forests of the Middle Atlas and see Barbary monkeys on the way, who live in a beautiful cedar forest. After Lunch will in Midelt, we will drive through several typical villages and explore nomad families living in their tents along the road. We carry on through the Ziz Valley and reach Merzouga Sahara Desert, a sea of windblown dunes. The customary welcome awaits you at Erg Chebbi, a glass of mint tea and the hospitality of your hosts. Enjoy the scenery and ready yourself for the journey to the desert campsite. We drive to camp either by camel riding, 4x4 or quads, get settled and relax in the tranquility of this very special place. After dinner, we relax fireside under a canopy of stars.
Day 3: Merzouga Desert-Todra Gorges- Tinghir- Ourzazate city- Ait Benhdou Kasbah- Ounila Valley- Tichka pass- Marrakech:,
After breakfast, enjoy Sunrise on the way and camel trek back to Merzouga. Afterthat, we visit Rissani, a tradional market town and where a massive deposits of fossilized animals have been discovered. We carry on our drive to Todra Gorges via the Draa Valley .After lunch, we continue to Klaat Mgouna, known as The Valley of Roses. Our journey continues to visit Hollywood Studio and Ait Benhdou Kasbah, the UNESCO world Heritage Site. A number of films were made here like “Lawrence of Arabia”, “The Man Who Would Be King”, “The Mummy”, “Kingdom of Heaven”, “Gladiator”, and Babel”
Then, join the way that goes up to the winding mountain roads, (used Caravan Road that linked desert to Marrkech) to Tizi n'Tichka, taking in a stark and beautiful terrain. The road takes us through several isolated Berber villages situated along the mountainside and below in secluded gorges. It's a fascinating and realistic glance at the landscape and culture of these rugged and hospitable people. Lunch will be enjoyed on the pass. Our final destination is Marrakech, where we the tour ends.
