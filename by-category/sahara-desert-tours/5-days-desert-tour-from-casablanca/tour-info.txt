ITINERARY:
Day1 : Casablanca-Rabat-Meknes-Fes ,
You'll be picked up at 8:00 am from your place in Casablanca. First, you'll visit the impressive Hassan II Mosque, a famous landmark with the tallest minaret. Then, explore the scenic Corniche Ain Diab. Afterward, head to Rabat, the political capital, and visit the Oudaya Kasbah—a historic area with charming narrow streets and blue houses. Explore the Chella Necropolis, where Roman and Islamic ruins coexist, telling stories from centuries ago. Continue your journey to the ancient Roman ruins of Volubilis before reaching Fes, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its historical importance. Spend the night here.
Day2 : Fes- Atlas Mountains- Ziz valley- Merzouga Desert ,
Picking you up from your riad or hotel after breakfast for a journey to Merzouga. The scenic route leads through the Middle Atlas, passing by Irfan, Midelt, Errachidia, Erfoud, and Rissani. A lunch break will be arranged in a suitable location based on the current weather. Throughout the day, you'll traverse forests, rugged rocks, and Berber villages, gradually descending from the Atlas Mountains into the Sahara Desert. Around noon, there will be a lunch stop in the city of Midelt, renowned for its fossils and rocks. Following that, you'll continue the journey, taking in the changing landscapes. Upon reaching the Kasbah of Merzouga, you'll have the opportunity to freshen up with a hot shower or enjoy a refreshing dip in the pool, depending on the weather conditions. This will be a delightful break after a day of travel. The evening concludes with a satisfying dinner served at the Kasbah.
Day3 : Guided sightseeing tour in Merzouga,
After breakfast, embark on a visit to the village of Khamliya, home to the Gnawas, people of African origin who have settled in the Moroccan desert in pursuit of a better life. Explore the village and gain insights into their unique way of life. Next, head to the city of Rissani for a tour of its bustling souk, where you can immerse yourself in local products. Don't miss the opportunity to savor a Berber pizza known as "medfouna" at a local restaurant. In the afternoon, get ready for a camel ride across the dunes, unveiling the secrets of the vast sea of sand. Your destination is a pristine oasis where you'll have a chance to relish the tranquility of the desert, marvel at the beauty of nature, and witness a breathtaking sunset. The evening unfolds with dinner and an overnight stay in a Berber tent, situated at the base of one of the tallest dunes in Erg Chebbi. It promises to be a serene and enchanting experience under the desert sky.
Day4 : Merzouga- Toudra gorges- Dades Gorges- Ouarzazate ,
Enjoy an early breakfast and a revitalizing shower before embarking on your journey to Ouarzazate via the Toudra Gorges. The route, spanning 14 km from the town of Tinghir, will guide you through Boumalen Dades, the charming Kalaat Megouna renowned as the Valley of Roses, and Skoura. Your final destination is Ouarzazate, where you'll find dinner and overnight accommodations at the hotel. This leg of your adventure promises a continued exploration of diverse landscapes and cultural encounters.
Day5 : Ouarzazate- Ait Ben Haddou Kasbah- Atlas Mountains- Marrakech ,
After a satisfying breakfast, commence your journey toward Marrakech. Before departing Ouarzazate, you have the option to explore the Kasbah of Taourirte, established by Pacha Laglaoui. If you're interested, a guided tour of the movie studios known as "50 Crazy People" is available, located 20 km from Ouarzazate. Another highlight is the renowned Ait Benhaddou Kasbah, positioned 9 km from the national road. The visit typically takes around an hour, allowing you to absorb the historical and architectural richness of this ancient fortress. Following the visit, proceed with your journey to Marrakech, enjoying picturesque landscapes en route. Take a break for lunch at a roadside restaurant. Upon arrival in Marrakech, you will be dropped off at your chosen destination, marking the conclusion of your desert adventure. It's a day filled with cultural exploration and scenic beauty as you transition from the desert to the vibrant city.
