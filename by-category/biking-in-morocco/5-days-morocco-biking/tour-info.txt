TOUR: 5 days Morocco biking
================================================================================

TOUR META:
  Duration: 5Days
  Price start from: $
  Location: Mountain bike tours

DESCRIPTION:
An incredible biking journey through the heart of Berber country will take you through the primary valleys of the Toubkal region. With the majestic Atlas Mountains as the backdrop, you will have the chance to immerse yourself in Berber culture and interact with the local population while you take in the unforgettable scenery of the Atlas Range scenery.

ITINERARY:
DAY1, MARRAKECH
We will greet you at Menara Airport and bring you directly to your hotel. If you have travelled with your bike, we'll take it to the shop to be mounted and store your box until you depart. For convenience purposes we've chosen a 3*** hotel close to the bike shop which offers nightly accommodation with breakfast included.
DAY2, KIK PLATEAU
Your guide and driver will meet you at the hotel in the morning. You'll be transported by minibus to an area near Lake Takerkoust, about 45 km southwest of Marrakech. We'll begin our ride on after checking our bikes and gear; we'll start our journey on fairly flat ground, and then gradually ascend to the village of Taghenchoute. From this point we zigzag up to the Kik plateau, which gives a really fantastic view of the majesty of the Atlas Mountains and Jebel Toubkal, at 4167 m, the highest peak in North Africa, and the jewel in the crown of this mighty range. The Kik Plateau is beautiful throughout the year, changing its colors with the seasons: from the many shades of springtime greens to the golden yellows of summer, the russet reds and browns of autumn and the pristine white of winter snowfall on the mountains. Our ride takes us through the terraced mountainsides and local Berber villages, descending into Asni from altitude pretty quickly. If you're in the mood to keep going, we can continue for the remaining 14km on tarmac, arriving in Imlil. If not, we load our bikes onto the 4x4 and take a relaxing ride to Imlil where we'll overnight in a local guesthouse. Breakfast, lunch & dinner included. Distance riding: 32 km. Altitude gain/loss: 1047m/580m. Minimum/maximum altitude: 460m/1460m.
DAY3, Imlil-Ounasekra-Asni
We begin the day's ride in the foothills of Mount Toubkal, ascending the challenging Tizi n'Tamatart at 2230 m. atop this high mountain pass we can take in the stunning views of the High Atlas, frequently snow-capped and always majestic. From Wanskra village, we'll continue via a dirt track winding down through the valley, and overlooking the deep riverbed (wadi), and terraced mountainsides dotted with villages that seem to dangle precariously. We'll overnight in Asni, where the ride terminates, enjoying dinner and welcome rest in a local lodge.
Biking distance: 36km. Altitude Gain/Loss: 1020km/1600km. Minimum/Maximum altitude: 1160km/2345km.
DAY 4, OURIKA VALLEY * MARRAKECH
Today we test our mettle, setting out for a brief warm-up ride on flat terrain and then pushing ourselves on the ascent to Tadmant Pass, at 1800 m. this is a long and somewhat arduous ride We will be rewarded for this challenging climb with fabulous views of the breadth of the region, from the northern plains to the mighty Atlas mountains. Our trail continues east into the lush Ourika Valley. Here our day ends and our team will load our gear and drive us the short distance back to your accommodation in Marrakech. Breakfast & lunch provided. Biking distance: 36 km. Altitude Gain/Loss: 1120 m/970 m. Minimum/Maximum Altitude: 1160 m/1865 m.
DAY 5,
Breakfast is provided, as well as transport from your hotel to Menara Airport.
