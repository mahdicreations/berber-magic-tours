ITINERARY:
Day1 : Fes- Ifrane- Azrou- Midelt ,
After having breakfast, our journey will take us to Midelt via Ifrane, often referred to as the Switzerland of Morocco. En route, we will reach the Azrou forest, where you'll have the opportunity to witness and enjoy the monkeys as they play and climb on the cedars. Continuing our drive through the scenic mountains of the Middle Atlas, we'll finally arrive in Midelt for our stay at the hotel. It's a day filled with picturesque landscapes and a delightful encounter with the playful monkeys in the Azrou forest.
Day2 : Midelt- Ziz Valley-Merzouga Desert ,
After breakfast, our journey will lead us through the Ziz Valley and its picturesque gorges, making our way to Errachidia. From there, we'll head to Erfoud, renowned as the capital of fossils. Continuing our exploration, we will proceed to Merzouga, where the day will be dedicated to immersing ourselves in the serene atmosphere of the desert. You'll be accommodated at a hotel near the dunes, providing a comfortable and convenient stay as you soak in the tranquility of the desert surroundings.
Day3 : Guided tour in Merzouga Desert ,
After enjoying breakfast, our day will commence with an extensive tour of the desert, taking us through several villages nestled in the dunes. One notable stop on our journey is the village of Khamlia, where we'll pause to immerse ourselves in the captivating rhythms of Gnawa music. Continuing our route, we will visit the desert nomads in their homes, providing a unique insight into their way of life. As the afternoon unfolds, we'll embark on a camel ride to reach a haima nomadic camp, nestled in the heart of the dunes. This will be our overnight accommodation, allowing you to experience the magic of the desert under the open sky. It promises to be a day filled with cultural encounters, scenic beauty, and the enchanting atmosphere of the Sahara.
Day4 : Merzouga- Rissani-Tinghir-Toudra Gorges ,
Early in the morning, we'll rise to witness the breathtaking sunrise and climb the dunes, offering a spectacular view of the desert landscape. Following this, we'll ride on camels back to the hotel for a delicious breakfast. Our journey will then continue towards the Toldra Gorges, passing through Rissani along the way. In Rissani, we'll take some time to explore its traditional souk, renowned as the largest in southern Morocco. Continuing our route, we'll reach the Toldra Gorges, where we'll spend the night at a hotel. It's a day filled with the magic of the desert sunrise, camel rides, cultural exploration in Rissani, and the scenic beauty of the Toldra Gorges.
Day5 : Toudra Gorges- DadesGorges- Skoura Oasis- Ouarzazate ,
After having breakfast at your hotel, we'll take a short walk through the gorges before continuing our journey to the Dades Valley and the Rose Valley. In the Skoura Oasis, we'll make a stop to explore one of the oldest kasbahs in southern Morocco, dating back 700 years, known as Kasbah Ameridil. Afterward, we will head to Ouarzazate to visit the ancient Kasbah of Taourirte, classified as a World Heritage site, and the Museum of Cinema. You will be accommodated at your hotel in Ouarzazate, concluding the day filled with exploration, historical sites, and the unique charm of Morocco's landscapes.
Day6 : Ouarzazate- Ouarzazate Studio- Ait Ben Haddou- High Atlas Mountains-Marrakech ,
After breakfast and before proceeding to Marrakech, we will explore the Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou, a renowned location where numerous films have been shot, especially those set in the Sahara, including The Kingdom of Heaven, Cleopatra, and Gladiator. Following our visit to the Kasbah, we will continue our journey towards Marrakech, crossing the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi N'tichka pass. During this scenic drive, you'll have the opportunity to admire the picturesque landscapes, including Berber villages and farms along the way. We'll arrive in Marrakech in the afternoon, where you will be accommodated in a riad. It's a day that combines cinematic history at Ait Ben Haddou with a breathtaking drive through the High Atlas, culminating in the vibrant atmosphere of Marrakech
