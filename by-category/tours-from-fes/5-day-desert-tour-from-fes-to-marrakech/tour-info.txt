ITINERARY:
Day1 : Fes- Ifrane- Azrou- Eerfoud-Merzouga Desert ,
At 8 am, we'll pick you up from your hotel for a journey to Merzouga, passing through the Middle Atlas, a 350-kilometer mountain range extending from southwest to northwest. Along the way, we'll traverse cities such as Ifrane, known as the "Moroccan Switzerland" for its picturesque architecture, and Azrou, a renowned Berber city in Morocco. The route will take us through the valley and gorges of Ziz, passing cities like Midelt and Erfoud.
Once in Erfoud, we'll take a desert track leading to Merzouga. The day's adventure will culminate in spending the night at a hotel located near the entrance to the desert. It promises to be a journey filled with diverse landscapes and the exploration of notable cities, setting the stage for the desert experience awaiting you in Merzouga.
Day2 : Guided tour in Merzouga Desert ,
On the second day, our focus will be on immersing ourselves in the most breathtaking landscapes of the Eastern Sahara. We'll embark on a 4X4 adventure, exploring nomadic villages, encountering oases, and navigating the impressive dunes of Erg Chebbi. Along the way, we'll have the opportunity to visit the renowned village of Khamlia, home to the Gnawa people—descendants of African slaves. Here, we'll be treated to their captivating folk music.
As the day transitions to dusk, we'll return to the hotel to mount camels, which will lead us to a Berber camp nestled in the heart of the dunes. Here, we'll dine under the desert sky and spend the night, surrounded by the tranquility and beauty of the Sahara. It's a day filled with exploration, cultural experiences, and the enchanting allure of the desert.
Day3 : Merzouga- Rissani-Tinghir-Todra Gorges- Dades Gorges ,
Start your day by waking up early to witness the enchanting sunrise over the desert. Following this, embark on a camel ride back to the dunes. After enjoying breakfast and a refreshing shower, our journey continues as we drive to Rissani and Erfoud. A visit to the fossilized marble factory will provide insight into the local craftsmanship. We then proceed to the palm groves of Touroug and Tinjdad.
Our lunch stop will be at the stunning Todra Gorge, surrounded by breathtaking landscapes. In the afternoon, the journey continues to the Dades Gorge via Boumalne. As the day concludes, we'll have dinner and spend the night at a hotel, ensuring you experience the unique charm of the region. It's a day filled with natural wonders, cultural exploration, and the tranquility of the Moroccan landscape.
Day4 : Gorge Dades- Kelaa M’Gouna- Skoura- Ourzazate ,
After breakfast, we trace our way back to the Dades Valley and set off on the road to Boutaghrar, where nature's myriad colors converge. Our journey unfolds along the route of a thousand kasbahs, weaving through the kasbahs, ksours, the Valley of Roses, and the Skoura palm grove. Along the way, there's an option to explore the Kasbah Amredhyle. Our next destination is the famous Kasbah Aït Benhaddou, a UNESCO World Heritage site, where we'll have the chance to explore its historic and cultural significance.
As the day comes to an end, we proceed to spend the night in a hotel in Ouarzazate. It's a day filled with vibrant landscapes, historical exploration, and the unique charm of the kasbahs and valleys of Morocco.
Day5 : Ourzazate- Ait Ben Haddou- Marrakech ,
Explore the city renowned for its kasbahs, including the Taourirt World Heritage Site and one of the most beautiful palaces in the region. Not far from the city is the Kasbah of Ouarzazate, where we'll pause to visit the cinema museum and film studios, known for hosting many international films such as Gladiator, Lawrence of Arabia, Game of Thrones, and Babel. After this cinematic exploration, we'll travel 20 km and turn right to visit the famous kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou.
Following the visit, our journey continues as we cross the High Atlas Mountains, revealing spectacular landscapes and hidden Berber villages nestled at the foot of immense valleys. There's an optional lunch stop at a local restaurant at the Tichka Pass. Alternatively, if you prefer to make the most of your remaining time, we'll proceed to Marrakech, providing an opportunity to visit the magnificent Majorelle Gardens. The choice is yours, ensuring flexibility and the chance to enjoy the aspects of Morocco that interest you the most.
