ITINERARY:
DAY1, Marrakech-Imi Oughlad-Gliz
Our journey begins in Marrakech, from here we transfer to Imi-Oughlad, about an hour and a half drive. Here we will meet our support team of muleteers and cook. With our gear loaded we make our way to Tizi Lbour and down to Imsker for lunch. Afterwards we'll trek along Ousterk Valley and their cool walnut groves, bypassing Tingharand Tiddli. At the village of Gliz we stop for the night. 5-6 hours walking
DAY2, GLIZ-OUKAIMDEN-TIZI N ADDI-TACHDIRTH
A short hike brings us out of Ousertk valley, up Tizi-n-Addi, through juniper forest and the lush surrounding scenery of the Atlas Range. Our journey continues over Okaimden Mountain, at a height of 3700 m. After we reach the ski station we will enjoy lunch, and from here continue toward the ridge (?) of Eddi. This is the gateway to the beautiful Imnan valley of the High Atlas. We spend the night in Tachddirt, the highest and largest village in the area. 6 hours walking
DAY3, TACHDIRTH-IKKIS-TIZI N AGUERSIWAL-MATAT
After a restful night we enjoy breakfast and begin our descent into Imnane Valley, passing through Berber villages such as Ouneskra, Tingherine, and Ikiss. From here we travel westward toward Tizi n'Ageursiwal, at 2000 m. After crossing the pass we descend to Agersioual village, following the mule path. Lunch is prepared and served alongside a river under the cool shelter of walnut trees. We continue then for an hour to Mattat valley where we will spend the night. 5 hours walking
DAY 4, MATTAT RED VILLAGE-TIZI OUDITE-AZIB N TAMSOULT
We begin our 6 hour hike out of Mattat Valley into Azzaden Valley. We will pass Tizi Oudite at 2250 m, Ait Aissa village, TIZI Oussm and continue into Azib n'Tansoult, where we will overnight in the mountain refuge at 2300m.
DAY 5, AZIB N TAMSOULT-TIZI AGELZIM-TOUBKAL REFUGE
Our climb today is a steady, somewhat arduous one, bringing us out of the Azzaden Valley, through Ighlouliden waterfalls, over Aguelzim Mountain pass at 3560 m. to the Toubkal Refuge known as Niltner But. Here you'll find yourself sharing bunk cabins with other hikers preparing for the ascent of Jebel Toubkal the following morning. 6 hours hiking
DAY 6 , TOUBKAL REFUGE -TOUBKAL SUMMIT-IMLIL-MARRAKECH
We begin our day early for the ascent of Jebel Toubkal, North Africa's second highest peak. Conditions allowing, your journey to the summit will reward you with breathtaking views from the Atlas Mountains to the edge of the Sahara. We descend the well-worn path from the summit down to base camp and on to Imlil. It's an active and long day with 8-9 hours hiking, a 900m ascent and 2500m descent. From Imlil you will be transported back to Marrakech around 5:00 p.m.
