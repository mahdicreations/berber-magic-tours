ITINERARY:
Day 1: Marrakech - Imlil valley- Toubkal base camp,
You will be picked up from your accommodation in Marrakech. Drive towards the high Atlas Mountains. It will take around 1h30 drive to get to Imlil valley which is the starting point from your lodge in Imlil where you will spend the night.
Then, you will start trekking along the Ait Mizane Valley. First towards the village of Aremd and then onto the shrine of Sidi Chamarouch, which attracts tourists and pilgrims alike. The village sits beside a small waterfall, a jumbled cluster of houses that seem to melt together into an anarchic mass. You will have a stop for lunch. After that, from here the trail continues to climb steadily and zigzagging its way up to the Toukbal Refuge (3206m), our stop for the night. It will take around 5 to 6h walking.
Day 2: Refuge of Toubkal - Toubkal summit - refuge,
After an early breakfast, you make your attempt on the summit of Jebel Toukbal. Your route takes you up the south cirque, crossing the stream above the refuge and then heading across one of the mountains numerous scree fields. The walking is relatively straightforward, but the views along the way make the journey more than worthwhile. Once you get to the top, you will enjoy amazing views over the high Atlas Mountains. After a short break on the pass, you will return back via the same route to the refuge where you will have lunch and overnight again. It will take around 5 to 6h walking.
Day 3: Base camp- twin peaks-Imlil valley,
Today after breakfast, you will climb another two peaks at the altitude of 4086m & 4089m peaks which are called Timzguida & Ras n’Ouankrim. It’s uphill & a bit technical at few points where you will use your hands. Once you reach these peaks , you will admire wonderfull views. After a short break to take pictures, you will get back via the same route to the basecamp where you will have lunch. Then, heading down to the last village of Imlil where you will spend the night. Today’s trek is 8-9hours walking.
Day 4: Imlil - Marrakech :,
We start our walk down visiting some villages, passing through the waterfalls and the gardens while enjoying the views of the valley and the cultivated gardens. A delicious lunch will be prepared for you in a beautiful spot in the nature where you will relax.
After that, you will be transported back to your accommodation in Marrakech. You will be here during the evening.
