ITINERARY:
Day1: Marrakech -Atlas mountains - Imlil valley - Tizi Mzik,
The transport will pick you up from Marrakech to Agersioual village, passing by Tahnouate, Moulay Brahim Gorges and Asni.You will be in Imlil After 1h20mins of driving.Here, you start your trek at 1600m up to the pass of Tizi Mzik (2400m), a beautiful scenery provivded over Azaden valley , Imlil valley and other mountains like Oukaimdn, a famous ski resort in Africa. Lunch time will give us the opportunity to relax in the open calm green spaces with surprising views.Then, heading down to Armed village , 2000m where you spend the first night, the highest village in Imlil valley. We will take to a Berber house here to have tea and cake.
The day’s trek is 4-5hours walking.
Day 2: Armed village-Sidi chamharouch,
After breakfast, we start walking along the valley of Toubkal through a beautiful juniper forest and surprising scenery along the way till we get onto to the shrine of Chamharouch . Here, you stop to have break and enjoy your lunch beside the river. After, we comeback down and appreciate the more views. Once we get to the village, you will have tea with crepes ,jam and honey. After dinner , you will have a Hamam ( a traditional sauna ) with massage, highly recommended ( optional ).
Day3 : Imlil valley - Kik plateau - camel riding or quads in desert Agafay- Marrakech ,
Today, you head down though the valleys of Atlas mountains, visit the waterfalls of Imlil valley. Lunch will be under the walnuts trees in a green space, calm and beautiful. Then, the transport will take us to plateau of Kik ( well-known for white fields and at this time will be green) , pass by Agergour spot of paragliding to desert Agafay. Here you have two options or enjoy both( up to you) :
Either to have an enjoyable quad bike tour through the rocky Desert.
Or camel riding in the rocky desert. Then, Appreciate more landscape , then, have a stop on the route to enjoy a typical Moroccan mint tea.
After that, you will make it back to Marrakech where you overnight.
Note: there is possibilty to have dinner in desert Agafay. Overnight in Marrakech or desert Agafay.
