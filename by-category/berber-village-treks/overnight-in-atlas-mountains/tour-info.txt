ITINERARY:
Day 1: Marrakech-Imlil valley-Armed village:,
Travel from Marrekch to Armed village, a tradional Berber village nestled in the foothills, is the most popular gateway to the majestic High Atlas Range. It is close to Jbel Toubkal, it is the perfect bade for day-trekking through the area, visiting neighboring villages such as Imlil, waterfalls and its lovely example of terrraced farming. If your goal is a stroll through the foothills and taking in the stunning scenery of the area, you couldn’t pick a better place.
From Imlil center which offer an abundance of breathtaking views of the Atlas Mountains and surrounding landscapes, it is where we will stop to have a cup of tea in a Berber Caffee. We then discover some beautifull villages and watterfalls of the valley.
At Armed village, we enjoy an authentic and delicious Berber meals at the Armed Berber house, taking in the amazing views of Toubkal peak, interact with locals and enjoy a wonderfull night under the mountains skies and its stars.
Day2: Imlil valley-Imlil Marrakech:,
Today, you will enjoy leisurely walk through the villages and the cultivated gardens of the area which provides some really remarkable views of the High Atlas.
In a peaceful setting under the wallnut trees outside of the villages, lunch will be prepared by your cook. The day’s journey concludes in the afternnon, and from Imlil you will be transported back to your hotel in Marrakech.
