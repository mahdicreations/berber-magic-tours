Tour Overview

A variation on the theme, this 3-day trek is another great introduction to the landscapes and native settings that await you in the High Atlas Mountains. Pastoral settings, rugged peaks and gentle hills are the settings for our passage through this very unique and spectacular region.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

MARRAKECH-IMLIL-TINGHOURIN

After breakfast in Marrakech, we travel to Imlil where we will rendezvous with your guide and mule team for the 3 day journey. We travel first toward Tineghourhine, via Tamatert. Lunch time will give us the chance to relax in the cool green spaces of Tizi 'n Tamatert (2279m). After the lunch break we continue to Ouanesekra-Tamguist, ending the day's hike at Tineghourhine (1600m.), where the night is spent in a local gite. The trek is 4 hours.

Day 2

TINGHOURINE-IKKIS-ID AISSA

After breakfast in Tinerghourhine we will trek along the Imnane Valley, passing the village of Ikkiss (1700m.) In Ikkiss you will have a photo break so that you can be certain to capture some of the memories of Berber terrain and customs. From here we continue the trek toward Tizi n'Aguersioual (2000m.), where lunch will be eaten in Matate village (2000m.). The day's hike is 6-7 hours, after which we will spend the night in a local gite in Id Aissa (1800m.)

Day 3

ID AISSA-IMLIL-MARRAKECH

During today's trek we will visit Toubkal National Park, taking in the majesty of the highest waterfalls in the region. We then meet the path which takes us over Mzik Pass (2500m.), offering us astounding views of the Ait Mizan Valley. After enjoying lunch in Mzzik we hike down to Imlil, and transport is provided back to Marrakech.

About The Trek

Essential details, accommodation options, and gear recommendations to help you prepare for your journey with Berber Magic Tours.

At Berber Magic Tours, we believe every great adventure starts with expert planning, honest advice, and exceptional local service. Our experienced team and licensed mountain guides are passionate about sharing the beauty of Morocco’s High Atlas Mountains, Mount Toubkal, and Berber culture with travelers from around the world.

Our certified guides have years of experience leading treks and receive ongoing safety training to ensure your journey is both enjoyable and secure. They are supported by skilled muleteers and well-cared-for mules that transport luggage and camping equipment, allowing you to hike comfortably while enjoying spectacular mountain scenery.

Throughout your trek, you’ll enjoy freshly prepared Traditional Moroccan Berber Meals, reliable logistics, and personalized support. We also provide practical guidance on weather conditions, water, seasonal changes, and trekking essentials, helping you stay prepared in every season. Whether you’re climbing Mount Toubkal, exploring traditional Berber villages, or embarking on desert tours, our goal is to deliver an authentic, safe, and unforgettable trekking experience while supporting local communities through responsible tourism.

Berber Guesthouses

Experience authentic Berber hospitality by staying in a traditional guesthouse in the High Atlas Mountains. The accommodation is simple, clean, and comfortable, featuring cozy beds with foam mattresses. During your stay, our experienced cook prepares delicious homemade Moroccan meals using fresh local ingredients, giving you a true taste of Berber cuisine.

Camping

For those seeking a closer connection with nature, camping is available from June to October. We provide spacious, modern igloo-style tents designed for two people, and larger tents can be arranged for taller guests when requested in advance. Each campsite also includes a traditional Berber tent where guests can enjoy freshly prepared meals, relax, and share stories after a day of trekking. If you need accommodation before or after your trek, we can arrange a stay at our village.

Refuge

For high-altitude treks, the mountain refuge offers a warm and comfortable place to rest. Facilities include hot showers, Western-style toilets, a heated lounge with electricity, and dining areas where our team serves freshly cooked meals. To keep the refuge clean and comfortable for everyone, hiking boots are left outside, so we recommend bringing lightweight shoes or sandals for indoor use.

Preparing the right clothing and equipment will make your trek in the High Atlas Mountains safer and more comfortable. Weather conditions can change quickly, especially at higher elevations, so we recommend dressing in layers and bringing gear suitable for both warm days and cold mountain evenings.

Clothing for Trekking

  • Wear comfortable, well-broken-in trekking boots or lightweight hiking shoes with good grip.
  • Pack warm clothing, including a down or insulated jacket, a waterproof jacket, thermal base layers, fleece or wool sweaters, trekking trousers, and moisture-wicking T-shirts.
  • Don’t forget warm socks, gloves, a wool hat for cold mornings, and a sun hat or cap for daytime hikes.
  • Lightweight shoes or sandals are also recommended for relaxing at the guesthouse, mountain refuge, or campsite after each day’s trek.

Equipment for Trekking

Bring a trekking duffel bag or backpack for your main luggage and a comfortable daypack for essentials such as water, snacks, extra clothing, and your camera. We also recommend carrying a sleeping bag, reusable water bottles, sunglasses, trekking poles, a headlamp with spare batteries, sunscreen, lip balm, a basic first-aid kit, and a money belt for valuables.

Note: If you don’t have your own trekking gear, there’s no need to worry. Most essential equipment can be rented or purchased in Imlil at affordable prices, and our team will be happy to recommend reliable local outdoor shops before your adventure begins.
