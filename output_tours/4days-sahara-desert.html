Tour Overview

Embark on an unforgettable adventure for 4 Days, 3 Nights with Berber Magic Tours. Discover authentic Berber villages, stunning mountain landscapes, and rich local culture in the High Atlas Mountains. Our experienced local guides will ensure a safe, scenic, and immersive journey tailored for all nature and culture lovers.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Marrakech - Ait Benhdou - Ourzazate city -Dades valley,

Today, the day's journey starts from your accommodation in Marrakech and join the way that goes up to the Atlas Mountains though the winding mountain roads to Tizi n'Tichka, taking in a stark and beautiful terrain. The road takes us through several isolated Berber villages situated along the mountainside and below in secluded gorges. It's a fascinating and realistic glance at the landscape and culture of these rugged and hospitable people. Once down from the mountain passes we follow the Road of a Thousand Kasbahs to the city of Ouarzazate. Here we enjoy lunch and afterward continue to Klaat Mgouna, known as The Valley of Roses. From here you can take in some remarkable views of the valley, kasbahs, and the southern side of the Atlas range. Our final destination is Dades, where we will overnight.

Day 2

Dades gorges- Merzouga desert,

After breakfast, we depart Dades and head toward Todgha Gorge. Both the Dades and Todgha gorges are deep limestone canyons carved out by their respective rivers, in the eastern section of the Atlas Mountains. After a brief stop, we will continue to our luxury encampment at Auberge Plais Du Dunes, at Erg Chebbi, a sea of windblown dunes, some of which rise to a height of 150m. The customary welcome awaits you at Erg Chebbi, a gless of sweet mint tea and the hospitality of your hosts. Take your time here, enjoy the scenery and ready yourself for the journey to the desert campsite. We drive our camels to camp, get settled and relax in the tranquility of this very special place. After dinner, we relax fireside under a canopy of stars.

Day 3

Merzouga - Rissani City - Alnif - N'kob - Tazarine - Agdez - Ouarzazate,

Today, wake up early and climb the sand dunes to enjoy the beautiful sunrise. After breakfast & shower you will ride camel back to Merzouga town. We drive from Merzouga to Ouarzazate though Rissani (Tafilalt), where will walk around the famous, traditional and biggest Market of the area exploring the local-made goods and art crafts. It's better to visit Rissani in one of the following days: Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday. In these three days is souk, so it's good days for the people of desert area that want to do shopping. Continue the journey via Alinf, Tazarine and N'kob where we will stop for lunch.

In the afternoon, we will drive along the beautiful Draa Valley, lined with old Kasbahs, Berber villages and palm groves. Our tour continues to Ouarzazate through Agdez town and crossing the Anti-Atlas Mountains (1660m). In the evening we'll arrive to Ouarzazate, Dinner and overnight will be in a Hotel.

Day 4

Ouarzazate - Atlas Studios - Ait Benhaddou Kasbah - Ounila valley - Telouet village - High Atlas - Marrakech,

After breakfast, we will visit Atlas Studios in Ouarzazate known as the Hollywood city in Morocco. After that, we will go to Kasbah of Ait Benhaddou, a fortified walled Kasbah built right on the caravan path between Marrakech and the Sahara. Inside the high mud walls are six Kasbahs and a small number of homes. Ait Benhaddou has appeared in several movies, including Gladiator, Lawrence of Arabia, and Games of Thrones. We will then drive to Marrakech via the stunning Ounila valley and Telouet village. Later, we will pass through the high point of Tizi N'tichka pass (2260m). We will be in Marrakech in the evening and drop you off on your accommodation.
