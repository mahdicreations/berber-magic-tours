Tour Overview

2-day trek is another great selection of introduction to the kingdom of landscapes and origin native settings that await you in the High Atlas. It will take you discovering the Berber Villages and Valleys with its endless terrains, rolling into the distance as far as the eye can see. The Atlas Mountains in the National Park of Toubkal provides a valuable water sources to this valleys and therefore attracts wildlife species such as trees: juniper, pine, cherry and walnut, which provides its travelers with enough shade throughout the trek. And thanks to this unique park.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Marrakech - Imi oughlad- Pass Tacht - Tizi Oussem 1950m.

After breakfast in Marrakech, we travel to Imlil of 1H30 driving from Marrakech to Imi Oughlad. Here, you will get to know your guide and mule team. When your stuff are loaded, we will begin our trail ascending up to Tizi Tacht(2005m) which affords superb views of the Imlil and Azzaden valleys. Lunch time will give us the opportunity to relax in the open calm green spaces with surprising views of the Atlas Mountains. We then head down to the village of Tizi Oussem (1900m) via Tahliouine and Id Aissa villages while witnessing Berber people in their various daily activities . The day's trek is 6hours walking. Overnight in Berber house.

Day 2

Tizi Oussem - Tizi Mzik - imlil - Marrakech.

During today's trek, we will visit Toubkal National Park, taking in the majesty of the highest waterfalls (2300m) in the region. We then retracing the same way down, meet the path which takes us over Mzik Pass (2500m.), offering us astounding views of the Ait Mizan and Azzaden Valleys. After enjoying lunch in Mzzik, we hike down to Imlil, and transport is provided back to Marrakech. Today's trek is 5 hours walking.
