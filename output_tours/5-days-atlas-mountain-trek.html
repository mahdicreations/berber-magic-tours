Tour Overview

Embark on an unforgettable adventure for 5 Days, 4 Nights with Berber Magic Tours. Discover authentic Berber villages, stunning mountain landscapes, and rich local culture in the High Atlas Mountains. Our experienced local guides will ensure a safe, scenic, and immersive journey tailored for all nature and culture lovers.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Marrakech -Atlas mountains - Azzaden valley:,

The transport will pick you up from Marrakech to Agersioual village, passing by Tahnouate, Moulay Brahim Gorges and Asni.You will be in Agersioual After 1h30mins of driving.Here, you start your trek at 1600m up to the pass of Tizi Ouded (2000m), a beautiful scenery provivded over Azaden valley and other mountains like Oukaimdn, a famous ski resort in Africa. Lunch time will give us the opportunity to relax in the open calm green spaces with surprising views.Then, heading down to Tizi Oussem at 1850m where you spend the first night.

The day's trek is 6hours walking. Overnight in a Berber guest house.

Day 2

Tizi Oussem - Tizi Mzik - Armed village:,

During today's trek, we will visit Toubkal National Park, taking in the majesty of the highest waterfalls (2300m) in the region. We then meet the path which takes us over Mzik Pass (2468m.) offering us astounding views of the Ait Mizan and Azzaden Valleys. After enjoying lunch in Mzzik, we hike to the largest and last village in Imlil Valley ( Armed) . Today's trek is 7 hours walking.

Day 4

Armed village-Sidi chamharouch :,

Today, you will have the chance to explore more nature. You will do another gentle tour that's to a great place where the goats , sheeps and mules seek grass and water during the day while enjoying the landscape of Atlas mountains, it's flora and fauna. Then, you continue passing through a pine tree forest from where you will see Toubkal summit, the other peaks and Imlil valley from a distance. Then, you head down passing by a waterfall , the gardens. Then, overnight in Armed and enjoy more delicious meals.

Day 5

exploring Imlil valley, gardens, market and waterfalls:,

After breakfast, you will enjoy the view of a variety of landscapes, mountain streams, valleys and villages. From here, we start our walk visiting some villages, passing through waterfalls while enjoying gorgeous landscapes. You will not the miss exploring the market and it's the staring point of many treks. Lunch will be in a beautiful spot under walnut trees in the valley. The transport will transport you back to Marrakech during the afternoon. There is a chance as well for you to do camel riding in desert agafay. You will be in Marrakech during the evening.
